# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback, and
do not harass anyone or share other people's private information. That includes phone numbers,
recordings and transcriptions.

## Scope

It applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents
the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/leoleiria/call-recording-downloader/security/advisories/new),
or by contacting [@leoleiria](https://github.com/leoleiria) directly. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove content and restrict participation, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
